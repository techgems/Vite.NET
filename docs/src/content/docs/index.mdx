## Why Vite.NET

Vite.NET bridges a Vite-powered front end (React, Svelte, Vue, Solid) and an ASP.NET Core
back end. Your SPA lives inside your .NET app, so it inherits your authentication, your
layouts, and your static-file pipeline — while Vite gives you a fast dev server, HMR, and
optimized production bundles.

## What your Vite integration looks like

An integration has three moving parts:

1. **A Vite plugin** (`vite-dotnet`) added to your SPA's `vite.config.ts`. It configures the
   build output and emits a small JSON manifest describing the app.
2. **A NuGet package** (`TechGems.ViteDotNet`) registered in your ASP.NET Core app. It reads
   those manifests and exposes tag helpers.
3. **Two tag helpers** — `<dev-vite-scripts>` and `<prod-vite-scripts>` — that render the
   correct `<script>`/`<link>` tags into a Razor page for development or production.
